1. Introduction
Welcome to PexEdu!
This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application PexEdu, developed and published by Blacktree Studio.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This app is designed to be used without collecting, storing, or sharing any personal data.

2. Information We Do Not Collect
- We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.
- We do not track user activity.
- We do not use cookies or analytics tools.
- We do not show ads or use advertising identifiers.

3. Voluntary Information
The only time we may receive information from you is if you choose to contact us voluntarily by email (for example, to send feedback or ask for support).

In that case:
- Your email and message content are used only to respond to your inquiry.
- We do not share or sell this information to anyone.

4. Donations
PexEdu allows users to send voluntary donations to support the app.
We do not process payments directly — any donations are handled securely by the payment service provider you use (for example, Google Play or another third-party platform).

We do not collect or store your financial or payment information.

5. Third-Party Services
PexEdu does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
If, in the future, we add such services, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Children’s Privacy
PexEdu does not collect any data from users of any age.
The app is suitable for general audiences and does not contain content inappropriate for children.

7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally.
If we make significant changes, we will post the updated version at the same location and update the effective date above.
